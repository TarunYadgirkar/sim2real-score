sim2real-score — example reports

Two real runs, generated by the tool itself. Nothing here is hand-written.

sim2real-score takes a trained control policy, sweeps the physics it was trained under — friction, mass, damping, actuator gain, observation noise, action latency, sensor dropout — and finds where the policy stops working. It bisects each failure boundary and ranks the parameters by Sobol total-order index, so interactions count rather than one-at-a-time deltas.

Reading the score

The score is the share of the sampled randomization space in which the policy still meets its success criterion. It is not a percentage of anything real-world — it is only meaningful relative to the space you swept, which the report states at the top of each page.

The Sobol first-order indices (S1) in these two runs were computed at a low --sobol-base and include out-of-range estimates. Raise it before quoting them. The total-order (ST) column is the one to read.
